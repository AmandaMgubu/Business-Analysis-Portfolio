# Process improvement technique
## SREDIM Framework
The registration improvement endeavor was organized using the SREDIM framework as a disciplined cycle. The three most important issue areas causing most registration delays were document verification, payment confirmation, and student record generation, which I determined using the Pareto analysis in the Select step. By recording KPIs like average cycle time per registration, student record error rates, first-time-right verification percentages, payment reconciliation turnaround, and overall completion rates, I created a baseline performance during the Record stage. Moving into the Examine stage, I used the fishbone diagram and the Five Whys to analyze the underlying reasons. Problems include manual document management, unclear role ownership, and fragmented systems without APIs. A digital intake form with validation rules, a verification checklist aided by automation technologies, and system connectors to facilitate real-time data flow between the CRM, finance, and student management system were among the solutions I built during the Develop stage. During the Install stage, these enhancements were piloted in a single program, standard operating procedures were updated, staff members were trained, and students were given real-time progress updates. I included weekly KPI monitoring, monthly governance reviews, and automated data-quality checks in the Maintain stage to ensure that gains were maintained and reversion was avoided. Thus, SREDIM offered a methodical approach to problem identification, solution creation, and execution while guaranteeing ongoing accountability and monitoring through KPIs.
