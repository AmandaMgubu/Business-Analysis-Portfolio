# IMPROVEMENT APPLICATION AND FUTURE
The To-Be process is expected to be integrated, automated, and digitally first. Through a portal connected to the CRM, prospective students will submit their online applications. Their documents will be automatically validated, and the finance and admissions personnel will use the SMS to access synced records. Students will receive real-time status updates via SMS, email, and the portal, and fee quotations and confirmations will be generated automatically. The finance and admissions systems automatically create a student number when the payments are finished. There won't be any manual intervention or duplication because the process will be entirely trackable.


## Roadmap execution

### Stage 1 – Preparation stage (0 to 2 months)

•	Work with stakeholders to finalize requirements.

•	Obtain financing and contracts with IT vendors.

•	Inform employees of the project’s objectives and advantages.

### Stage 2 – Design stage (2 to 3 months)

•	Create a thorough BPMN for the To-Be procedure

•	Set up automation scripts and SMS-CRM connectivity

•	Decide on a test pilot group

### Stage 3 – Implement pilot stage (3 to 4 months)

•	Conduct a small-scale student pilot program for the new registration procedure

•	Track these KPIs: student feedback, error rate, and turnaround time

•	Resolve problems and improve processes 

### Stage 4 – Complete rollout stage (4 to 6 months)

•	Train academic, finance, and admissions personnel

•	Provide self-service tools for students, such as SMS alerts and a portal

### Stage 5 – Ongoing maintenance and Monitoring 
•	Keep an eye on your KPIs


•	To guarantee staff compliance and system stability, conduct reviews every 3 months 

•	As new requirements emerge, modify procedures


### Value sustainability

Sustainable value will be produced by this To-Be process by:

•	Shorter response times translate into quicker enrollment and more revenue flow

•	Increasing student satisfaction leads to increased reputation and retention

•	Improving operational efficiency results in less work for employees and fewer mistakes

•	University future proofing- integrated systems in line with the objectives of digital transformation


## Future state – BPMN Diagram

This future state BPMN diagram is a simplified and completely integrated student registration procedure that removes many of the inefficiencies found in the AS-IS model. The primary actors are Students, Admissions, Finance, and IT/SMS (Student Management System). The procedure starts when the student uses the portal/CRM to submit an online application. Electronic document verification is triggered instantly and carried out utilizing OCR or AI techniques. The system ensures accuracy as soon as possible by returning the case to the student for resubmission if any papers are missing or invalid. Following validation, the procedure moves to Finance, where the student can access a fee quotation automatically generated using the same site. After that, the student pays, and a decision gateway verifies that the payment was received and processed. A follow-up or aided assistance workflow is started to help the student if payment is not completed.

IT/SMS creates the student record, assigns a unique student number, and automatically enrolls the student in the modules of their choice if payment is confirmed. Real-time data integration between the IT, finance, and admissions systems guarantees that all parties involved are working with correct and consistent data throughout this process. Additionally, the IT/SMS system keeps students informed. It minimizes anxiety by sending proactive alerts (via email, SMS, and the online portal) at each significant milestone: application received, papers validated, costs quoted, payment confirmed, and final registration completed. The procedure is over when the student gets a confirmation and welcome message.

The BPMN model depicts a future state in which proactive communication, automation, and integration work together to provide a quicker, more dependable, and student-centered registration process.
