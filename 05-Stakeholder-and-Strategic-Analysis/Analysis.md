# SWOT Analysis
The SWOT analysis combines external factors (opportunities/threats) with internal reality (strengths/weaknesses). A digital mindset and a high demand for career-aligned programs were among our strengths; manual steps, inconsistent data, and siloed systems were our weaknesses; automation, self-service, funding integrations (like MICT SETA), and differentiation through quick onboarding were our opportunities; competitive institutions with more streamlined onboarding, compliance risks, and reputational harm from subpar administration were our threats. Leveraging our digital strengths to take advantage of a wholly integrated, self-service registration is one example of how I utilized SWOT to match process changes with strategy. I also treated weaknesses contributing to threats, such as data accuracy influencing compliance, as non-negotiable. By relating workflow automation and integration to risk mitigation, income conversion, and student experience, SWOT also assisted in defending investment.
